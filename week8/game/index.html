SIMPLE / NEW FOR THIS VIDEO

Court, ball, far player

One shared World core with the original ProxyKit / SimKit. The court is marked from representative frames of this video; far-player poses are sampled from this video. Ball paths, returns and range hits are design approximations, with no bounce and no full tennis scoring.

OURS / EXISTING ASSETS

Near player: v3 states and motions

8 states, 51 connection types, 229 stored seams. Skeletons and guide frames are read as stored; nothing is re-extracted, mirrored or interpolated at runtime. Seam skeletons are offline guides, not re-estimated evidence of naturalness.

NEW / GAIT AND DISPLACEMENT

Driven by the source trajectory, no stop on continuation

Per-frame left / right displacement is calibrated offline from the hip trajectory in the source video; seam speeds are smoothed offline. Left / right start, stop, reverse and continuation use this configuration while the skeleton still plays frame by frame. No contact correction or camera compensation, so stride length is not claimed to be accurate; strokes and other transitions keep a locked root.
