Lookup, input handling and asset limits

While moving you can request a forehand or backhand; during a stroke only the latest direction is kept, and the stroke cannot change type or be interrupted. With several arrow keys, the last pressed wins; losing focus clears the keys and pauses.

The 19 observed relations follow the original state machine. Connections the video does not cover, such as key release, free direction changes and serving from stationary, are labelled "Control supplement"; their transitions are also generated in advance, and runtime only looks them up and plays them. Canvas movement only indicates direction; it is not a court or stride simulation.

Curated clips have re-exported person masks checked at start, middle and end; racket and limb edges may still be incomplete. Both switches and same-state continuations play a stored 5-frame transition; the two images are aligned offline to one guide pose, and the page does not compute MLS. Movement exits are at most 3 frames apart; a transition in progress finishes first, and the page waits visibly if assets are not loaded. Ghosting or foot jumps can still appear.
