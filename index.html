How the latents are combined

Both variants pack one flat token sequence. They differ only in what sits in the conditioning slot ahead of the video being denoised.

(1)

Spatial-map conditioned

Trained on 1000 Pong clips. Control signal: the per-frame spatial map, a colourised mask of the moving entities only.

Test on Pong Held-out episode. style_6 is a style the model never trained on.

Test on Pac-Man The same Pong-trained model. No Pac-Man clip was in its training set.

(2)

Action conditioned

Trained separately on Pong and on Pac-Man, then each model tested on both games. Control signal: one action vector per frame — two channels of up / stay / down for Pong's paddles, a one-hot direction for Pac-Man.

Trained on Pong Tested on Pong, then on Pac-Man.
