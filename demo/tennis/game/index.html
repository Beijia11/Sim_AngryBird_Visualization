Match rules and controls

Play one set: 0 / 15 / 30 / 40, deuce and advantage. Win six games with a two-game lead; at 6–6 play a tie-break to seven with a two-point lead. The scoreboard updates after every point.

Serve from the right (deuce) court first, alternating sides each point. The ball lands in the diagonal service box and must bounce before the return. Players alternate serving each game; tie-break service is one point then two each. A fault allows a second serve; a double fault awards the receiver a point. The same player serves throughout a game. S starts your next point; when the agent is serving, its next points start automatically.

J / K queues a forehand / backhand. A moderate receiving area and a short timing grace help keep rallies going. Shots prepared too early expire, and the ball must still be in reach at contact. The ball continues on its fixed straight path during this window. Arrows move, Space pauses, R starts a fresh match.

Point and match endings use separate victory / disappointment reactions, with an existing raised-racket serve pose for the winner and a relaxed standing pose for the other player, animated in place. The original footage does not contain newly generated celebration clips. Serving targets are authored; this demo does not simulate net-cord lets or full line-judge officiating.
