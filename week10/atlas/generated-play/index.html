How does this page combine the earlier sections?

The players reuse the existing clips, pair graph and entry / core / exit rules: across states a clip is chosen from the usable pairs; a held input repeats only the current clip’s core. The far player is a local rule-based agent; the incoming ball’s target decides how it moves and swings.

Each shot first fixes a left / right direction and a landing point: serves land in the opposite service box, returns in the opposite back court. The ball’s ground projection always moves in a straight line; height separately shows the fall and one bounce. By default a serve takes about 1.11 s and a return about 1.22 s. No observed curve, no spin or slice, no physics simulation.

Speed, direction and landing are fixed at the hit; the receiving spot lies on the same line, so the ball never curves sideways to chase a player. The player must arrive before the ball; legal actions may play faster, and a late player misses: the ball never hovers and waits. Directions come from existing action–ball associations; the landing point is an interaction setting, not a measured landing in the source.

The source-video background stays still; the observations, labels and action pairs of the earlier pages are unchanged.
