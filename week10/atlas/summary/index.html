Data scope and fields

Reuses the action labels of both players and the reviewed ball paths of the four videos. The state at each moment is looked up strictly by the labelled intervals; uncovered moments show unknown. In the shot table, both states are taken at the centre of the associated action; the live panel always follows the playback time.

The incoming ball is the previous ball segment in the same rally associated with this action; the outgoing ball is the current one; without adjacent evidence it shows no association. The landing zone is a 2D estimate around the bounce; speed is camera-compensated image px/s, not km/h. Fast / slow grades use per-direction thresholds, so they are shown side by side only, with no inference of physical acceleration or cause.

This shows what happened in the source; it does not predict the ball path for a different action.
