Fixing the Control, and Testing the Agent

Two parts. The Cosmos-Edge fine-tune was being measured through a broken inference setting; this finds it and fixes it. Then the agent itself: one shot to a minimal-unit simulator, and a closed loop that asks whether its program can stand in for the video model.

Cosmos — the bug in the control

Every clip the fine-tuned Cosmos-Edge generated went dark one frame after the anchor and stayed dark. The players still followed the control, but the whole frame sat about 35 grey levels below the groundtruth, so foreground MAE was measuring brightness, not motion.

The cause is one inference setting: classifier-free guidance on the control branch. At text_guidance 3 and control_guidance 2 the sampler extrapolates six times along "with control minus without control". Plain Edge answers a seg control by copying the black control map, and a short fine-tune still leans that way, so extrapolating drags the frame toward black. The fix is to sample at control_guidance 1.0. Training is unchanged: training never uses guidance.

Agent — one shot to the minimal unit

Two things are expected of the agent. The first: given a video it has never seen, can it write, in one pass, a simulator whose state draws as the minimal unit a video model can render? The only requirement is that the mechanism is right. It does not have to look like anything.

Ten videos, two agents, no feedback round and no second chance: whatever world.js holds when the time budget ends is the result.

Agent — closed loop against the video model

The second expectation: with the evaluation in the loop, can the agent's own program replace the video model? That means all four at once — the mechanism right, the physics right, the appearance matching, and the motion natural, in a replay of the clip and in free play with inputs the video never contained.

Here the program renders its own pixels, no proxy. After the first build the evaluator scores the rendered frames, the lowest-scoring group becomes the next round's focus, and the agent gets up to five feedback rounds. A round is accepted only if its focus rises and no other group falls; a rejected round is rolled back, and two rejections in a row end the run. Fifteen minutes per round, at most six rounds.

What is measured

GroupMetricOnWhat it catches
A · appearancelpipsreplaythe frame as a whole does not look like the video
entity_dinoreplayan entity is not the thing it should be
entity_colorreplayright thing, wrong colours
background_lpipsreplaythe scene behind the entities is wrong
B · mechanismflow_epereplaymotion is in the wrong place from frame to frame
trajectory_adereplayentities do not take the paths they take in the video
event_f1replaycontacts and velocity jumps happen at the wrong times, or not at all
pose_dtwreplaybodies hold the wrong pose through the clip
C · naturalnesskinematicsreplay, free playjoints move at speeds and accelerations the video never shows
transitionsreplay, free playmotion snaps between poses instead of passing through them
bone_stabilityreplay, free playlimbs change length
foot_slidereplay, free playfeet slide while they are planted
joint_rangereplay, free playelbows and knees bend past what the video does
D · free playentity_consistencyfree playentities stop being themselves once the inputs are new
poppingfree playappearance jumps from one frame to the next
physicsfree playgravity does not match the video's
controllabilityfree playdifferent inputs give the same clip: nothing is actually controlled
not_copiedfree playframes are near copies of the video: it is replaying, not simulating
E · judgespotted_ratereplay, free playQwen3-VL picks the re-creation out of a (real, rendered) pair. 0.5 means it cannot tell. Reported, not used to accept a round

Nothing here is specific to one video or one domain: every metric is computed from entity boxes, tracks and human poses, which the measurement stage produces for any video. Pixel metrics are calibrated per video, scoring 1 at the video against itself shifted by one frame and 0 at a frozen first frame.

The time under each row is the whole closed loop: every agent round plus every evaluation. play opens the best round, rounds opens its report with the per-round times, metric changes, videos and code diffs.
