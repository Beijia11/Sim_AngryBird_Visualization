Causal Forcing++ on our seg-transfer Cosmos3-Edge

Teacher
  • bidirectional: every frame sees every frame
  • 35 steps × 2 forwards (text CFG 3)
  • 49.2 s per clip, nothing shown before the end
Student
  • frame-wise autoregressive, KV cache
  • 2 steps per latent frame, no CFG
  • 8.7 s per clip, first new frame after 2.7 s
in: seg control + GT first frame 121 frames, 25 fps → 31 latent frames latent 0 = GT anchor 390 tokens / latent frame 1458 fine clips control_guidance 1.0

\(x^i\): latent frame \(i\)  ·  \(c^i\): seg control of frame \(i\)  ·  \(y\): text  ·  \(\sigma=1\) noise, \(\sigma=0\) data

Stage 2 · Causal consistency distillation

35 steps × CFG → 4 or 2 steps, no CFG. Still trained on GT history.

grid and teacher step
\[ \sigma_n = \frac{5 s_n}{1 + 4 s_n},\quad s_n = 1 - \tfrac{n}{48},\quad n \in \{0..47\} \]
\[ x_{n+1} = x_n + (\sigma_{n+1}-\sigma_n)\big[\,v_\phi^{\varnothing} + 3\,(v_\phi^{y} - v_\phi^{\varnothing})\,\big] \]

\(\phi\): frozen Stage 1 model · \(\varnothing\): negative prompt

loss and EMA
\[ \mathcal L_2 = \Big\| \underbrace{x_n - \sigma_n v_\theta(x_n,\sigma_n)}_{\text{student } \hat x_0} - \operatorname{sg}\big[\underbrace{x_{n+1} - \sigma_{n+1} v_{\theta^-}(x_{n+1},\sigma_{n+1})}_{\text{EMA } \hat x_0}\big] \Big\|^2 \]
\[ \theta^- \leftarrow 0.99\,\theta^- + 0.01\,\theta \]
init: Stage 1 step 500, into all 3 netslr 2e-6, betas (0, 0.999) 8 passes / clip about 119 s / stepstopped after 500

Stage 3 · DMD with self rollout

train on its own history, exactly as at inference; judged on the whole clip by the bidirectional teacher.

self rollout
\[ \hat x^i = G_\theta\big(z^i \;\big|\; \hat x^{<i},\ c^{\le i},\ y\big), \quad i = 1..30 \]

gradient kept at one random exit step per frame · past K/V detached

DMD gradient and critic
\[ \nabla_\theta \mathcal L_{\text{DMD}} = \mathbb E_{\sigma,\epsilon}\!\left[ \frac{\hat x_0^{\text{fake}}(\hat x_\sigma) - \hat x_0^{\text{real}}(\hat x_\sigma)}{\operatorname{mean}\lvert \hat x - \hat x_0^{\text{real}} \rvert}\ \frac{\partial \hat x}{\partial \theta} \right] \]
\[ \mathcal L_\psi = \big\| v_\psi(\hat x_\sigma,\sigma) - (\epsilon - \operatorname{sg}[\hat x]) \big\|^2 \]

critic 4 : student 1 updates

init: Stage 2 step 500lr 2e-6, critic 4e-764 GB / GPU about 107 s / iteriter 750 = 150 student updates
